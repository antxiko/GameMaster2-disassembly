Tennis and Hockey

A two-player Pong (banks 7 and 8): there is no computer opponent. Everything runs inside the interrupt (p07:8004).

The controls

playersidejoystickkeyboard
1rightport 1arrows, space (A), SELECT (B)
2leftport 2E S F C, SHIFT (A), CTRL (B)

CTRL and C together go back to the start (p07:806E).

Tennis

It counts 15, 30, 40 and game, with no deuce or advantage (p07:9277). A set is 6 games with no need for a two-game lead, and the match is two sets. The serve changes every game.

Hockey

Whoever scores more goals wins (the scoreboard goes up to 99). The clock starts at 90 and goes down by one every 64 frames (p07:931A); at 10 there is a warning sound and there is a break, HALF TIME (p07:8367). At the end the one ahead wins, or it is a DRAW.

At the end of the match 1P WIN, 2P WIN or DRAW blinks (p07:831B).
