The texts

Everything the cartridge writes on screen, taken from the ROM with tools/saca_textos.py. There are four formats: [VRAM address] and letters with 0xFE / 0xFF (the menus); the same with the letters 0x20 below ASCII (Tennis and Hockey and the MSX biorhythm); [x, y] and letters (the MSX2 biorhythm), and loose strings ending in 0xFF. In the lists, "fila" is the row, "col" the column and "suelto" a loose string.

The signs follow the cartridge's own font (p00:483F), which is not ASCII: 0x3A is an arrow, 0x3D quotes, 0x3E the equals sign and 0x40 a dash. The typos are the ROM's own: "FORAMT" (three times), "UNFORMATED" and "BIORYTHM".
